Roger Pettingell. A reputation people can see.

A content project built around the person behind an established Sarasota real estate business.

“Jake took everything I built offline and made it visible online. Now it works for me 24/7.”

Roger Pettingell

The brief

Bring an established reputation online.

Roger’s work in Sarasota real estate gave him a story to tell long before the camera started rolling. The project brought that experience into an online presence that could introduce him before a prospective client’s first meeting.

The focus was Roger himself: how he works, what he knows about his market, and what clients can expect from him. Film and a personal-brand website give that story a place to live beyond a single social post.

Connected pieces

A story with more than one home.

  1. Film that introduces the person.

    A signature film gives people a way to get to know Roger’s experience and approach before a conversation. Watch the signature film on YouTube.

  2. A dedicated personal-brand website.

    A home for his story and business beyond a brokerage profile. Visit Pettingell Professionals.

  3. Content that keeps the conversation going.

    His public channels show the broader presence around the business. Explore Roger’s YouTube channel and Instagram profile.

Evidence and limits

What this project demonstrates.

Roger brought 38 years in real estate and more than $3 billion in career sales to the project. Those are Roger’s career credentials, not revenue attributed to Real Luxury Media.

The documented deliverables are a signature film, a dedicated personal-brand website, and connected public content. The links above let visitors review the work in its published context.

The testimonial on this page is attributed to Roger. This case study is evidence of one project and does not guarantee the same audience or business outcome for another client.
